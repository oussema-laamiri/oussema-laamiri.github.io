Liner notes

How I manage

  1. The 1:1 belongs to the engineer.Their agenda first, mine second.
  2. Decisions are written down.Architecture goes through open design reviews, so anyone can follow the reasoning later.
  3. Growth plans are concrete.One skill, one project to practise it, one date to look back.
  4. Feedback is small and frequent.It goes both ways, and nobody hears it for the first time in an annual review.
  5. Business first, then tech.I align the roadmap with the business, then let the team choose how to build it.
  6. Product and design are daily partners.I work with product managers and designers every day to align on priorities, scope and trade-offs, so engineering shapes what we build instead of only receiving it.
